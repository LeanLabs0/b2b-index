How B2B Index works

Methodology

Every ranking on this site can be explained in plain words. This page is the whole method: how companies get listed, how they rank, how categories launch, where the data comes from, and who owns the site.

checklistHow companies get listed

The minimum bar for any listing:

  • Verified legal entity. The company exists as a registered business, matched to public records.
  • Live domain. An operating website that matches the entity.
  • Published leadership. A named founder or executive, confirmed against a primary source.
  • Evidence on file. At least one primary-source URL backing the listed facts.

Inclusion is earned, never bought. Companies do not pay to be listed and cannot pay to be removed from a ranking position.

verifiedVerification tiers

Tiers are earned through evidence. Claiming a profile is free and is the path to the highest tier.

LISTEDThe entity is discovered and recorded with its basic facts.
check_circleCONFIRMEDFacts are matched to primary sources: the company site, public registries, leadership profiles.
verifiedVERIFIEDThe company claimed its entities.org record and proved ownership through its domain. The company maintains its facts; the research desk checks them.

Claiming happens once, on entities.org, the registry that powers every listing here. One claim updates the company's record across B2B Index and every sister property. No payment ever changes rank or score. Verification is free and open to every listed company.

functionsHow the ranking works

Rankings combine two things, and only dimensions with a live data source are scored:

  • Category fit — how squarely the company operates in the category, computed from its verified service and market footprint.
  • Verification depth — how much of the record is proven, and at which tier.

Anything we cannot back with data does not get a number. Review counts and ratings from other platforms are never republished; where relevant we link to the platforms directly.

radarHow categories earn a launch

A category index goes live only when two things are true: its companies are verified, and real buyer demand is mapped. Before launching a category we harvest the questions real buyers ask about it across search engines and AI assistants, so every index answers demand that actually exists.

campaignPaid inclusion policy

No payment ever changes rank or score. If a sponsored placement exists, it is labeled as sponsored and shown outside the numbered ranking. Any paid relationship is disclosed on the listing itself. There are currently no sponsored placements on B2B Index.

ruleCorrections

Every fact links to a primary source. If a company believes a fact is wrong, it can request a correction from its profile page (claiming is not required to request one). We verify against primary sources and correct within 7 business days. We correct records; we do not remove verified companies from rankings on request.

edit_noteEditorial

Category pages are assembled from the data sources above and reviewed before publishing. Editor: [Editor name, title]. The editorial gate is simple: if a fact has no primary source, it does not ship.

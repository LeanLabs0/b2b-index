How B2Bindex works

Methodology

Every ranking on this site can be explained in plain words. This page explains the score, the listing and verification process, and what money can and cannot buy. It also says who owns the site.

Preview with demonstration data.

functionsHow B2Bindex ranks companies

Each company gets a composite Index Score from 0 to 100, shown to one decimal (for example 98.4), plus a Health sub-score and a review star average. The Index Score is a weighted blend of five factors. Only factors with a live data source are scored; anything we cannot back with data does not get a number.

Company Authority Score25%

Scores the company across public knowledge properties such as Wikipedia, Wikidata, entities.org, and LinkedIn.

Industry Authority20%

Benchmarks domain and product authority against other providers in the category.

Brand Reputation20%

Aggregates verified reviews, case studies, and customer references from third-party sources.

Product Maturity(under review)20%

Measures how developed and proven the product or service offering is. This factor's definition is under review and may change.

Funding & Operational Health15%

Considers funding stage, runway indicators, and headcount growth as signals of stability.

What Health means. Health is a separate sub-score from 0 to 100. It reads operational signals only. Funding stage, runway indicators, headcount trend, and hiring activity. It is not part of the Index Score and carries no weight in it. The Funding & Operational Health factor uses some of the same signals but contributes only 15% of the Index Score. Health puts those signals on their own 0 to 100 scale.

Monthly snapshots. Every score recomputes in each one. Review counts and ratings from other platforms are never republished as our own; where relevant we link to the platforms directly. Reviewed by the B2Bindex editorial team.

checklistHow companies get listed

Basic listings are free. A company enters the index one of two ways:

  • Indexed by us. The editorial team records the company from public filings, licensed data providers, and other public sources. These profiles show as Unclaimed until someone at the company claims them.
  • Submitted by the company. Anyone at the company can submit a listing. The editorial team reviews submissions within 5 to 7 business days.

To appear in rankings, a profile needs a minimum completeness of 60%. Below that threshold the company stays listed but unranked until the missing facts are filled in.

verifiedVerification

Verification is B2Bindex's own. There are two states, and moving between them happens right here on the site:

UNCLAIMEDThe company is indexed from public data. No one at the company has claimed the profile yet. Every unclaimed row carries a Claim → link to Get Listed.
verifiedVERIFIEDThe company claimed its profile on B2Bindex and proved ownership through a work email on its own domain. Verified profiles are confirmed by the company and may complete missing data.

Verification carries a fee, one of only two paid programs on the site. Verification proves who you are. That's all it does.

campaignWhat money can and cannot buy

Two paid programs exist, and only two:

  • Profile verification. A fee to claim and verify a profile through the company domain.
  • Featured placement. Client placements in the Featured This Week module. It's labeled and sits outside the numbered ranking.

Paying doesn't move your rank. Rankings are computed from the five weighted factors above. The Brands to Watch module is selected by the data and cannot be bought. Any paid relationship is disclosed on the placement itself.

radarWhen a category goes live

A category index goes live only when two things are true: its companies are scored, and real buyer demand is mapped. Before launching a category we collect the questions real buyers ask about it, so we only launch categories people are actually searching.

databaseWhere the data comes from

Public filings Registrations, funding disclosures, and other public records
Licensed data providers Firmographics, headcount, and funding-stage data
Public knowledge properties The sources of record scored in Company Authority Score
Third-party reviews Verified reviews and references, linked to their platforms
Verified company submissions Facts maintained by companies on verified profiles
Demand mapping Buyer questions collected across search and AI answer engines

No source, no fact.

ruleCorrections

Every fact links to a source. If a company believes a fact is wrong, it can request a correction from its profile page (claiming is not required to request one). We verify against primary sources and correct within 7 business days. We fix wrong facts. We do not pull companies out of rankings because they asked.

How it works

STEP 1

Submit or claim

Tell us who you are and which company. If the company is already indexed, you claim its profile. If not, you submit a new listing. Claiming and submitting are both free. Verifying ownership through a work email on your domain is one of the two paid programs.

STEP 2

Editorial review

The B2Bindex editorial team reviews your submission within 5 to 7 business days. Listings need a minimum profile completeness of 60% to appear in rankings.

STEP 3

Rank

Your company joins its category leaderboard, scored on the same five weighted factors as every other company in the index.

Common questions

Does a listing cost anything?

The listing itself is free. There are two paid programs, verification and featured placement.

How do you verify ownership?

You confirm a work email on the company's domain. That is the whole proof. No documents, no phone calls.

How long does review take?

The editorial team reviews submissions within 5 to 7 business days. Corrections on verified profiles land within 7 business days.

What does Verified mean?

The company claimed its profile and proved ownership through a work email on its own domain. Every field on a verified profile is completed and confirmed.

What is a certified partner?

An agency certified by Brandvious to build profiles and publish sponsored content on the network. All sponsored content goes through certified partners, and it is always labeled.

Does a partner improve my ranking?

Not directly. A partner builds your profile and the evidence behind it. The score is computed the same way for every company.

How do I update my listing?

Claim the profile and edit it directly, or send a correction request from the profile page. Claiming is not required to request a correction.

What buyers ask

AnswerStack Q&A + citation stack

How much does a digital marketing agency cost per month?

Most B2B retainers land between $3,000 and $20,000 per month depending on scope. Project work runs $10,000 to $50,000. Fractional CMO engagements typically start near $5,000 per month.

Clutch · Digital Marketing Pricing Guide
"the majority of agencies bill monthly retainers in the $2,500 to $25,000 range"
HubSpot Blog · Agency Pricing Models
"value-based retainers are replacing hourly billing across mid-market agencies"
handshake Consensus: sources agree on retainer-first pricing; ranges vary by service depth.

What should a B2B SaaS company look for in an agency?

Named senior team, industry proof in your category, transparent reporting, and owned methodology. Verified founders and public case studies separate real operators from white-label resellers.

Gartner · Selecting a Marketing Agency
"buyers who verified agency leadership reported 2x satisfaction at renewal"
G2 Learn · Agency Buyer's Guide
"review recency matters more than review volume for services categories"
handshake Consensus: verification depth beats portfolio polish.

Agency vs in-house vs fractional: which fits when?

Under $1M ARR, fractional leadership plus contractors wins. $1M to $10M, a specialized agency accelerates without headcount. Past $10M, hybrid: in-house core with agency specialists.

Forrester · Marketing Org Design
"hybrid models now dominate B2B companies between $10M and $100M revenue"
MarketingProfs · Fractional CMO Trend Report
"fractional engagement grew 40% year over year in B2B services"
handshake Consensus: stage-dependent; no single model wins outright.

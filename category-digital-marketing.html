Digital & AI Marketing category FAQ

How are these rankings calculated?

Every agency gets a composite Index Score from five weighted factors, shown with their weights in the module above. The full detail is on the methodology page.

How often do scores refresh?

Scores refresh with each snapshot update. This category is awaiting its first computed snapshot, so growth and gain modules start populating from the next refresh.

What does the Verified badge mean?

Verified is B2Bindex's own status. It means the company claimed its profile and proved the details on it. Unclaimed rows show a Claim link.

How do you get your brand recommended in Google Gemini's AI answers?

Gemini recommends brands it can verify across several independent sources, so ranking well on Google is not enough on its own. The brand's details need to match on its own site, in its schema, and in the third-party pages that mention it.

How does growth-driven design work for B2B websites?

Growth-driven design launches a smaller site fast, then improves the pages that actually bring in leads as real visitor behavior comes in. A launchpad site of the highest-impact pages goes live in roughly 60 to 70 days instead of the four to six months a traditional B2B rebuild takes.

What is HubSpot CMS, and how does Content Hub work?

HubSpot Content Hub is HubSpot's content management system and AI-powered content marketing software for building a website and running the blogs, landing pages, and content around it in one place. It is the renamed and expanded version of what HubSpot sold as CMS Hub until an April 2024 rebrand.

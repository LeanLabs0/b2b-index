What buyers ask

How do you get your brand recommended in Google Gemini's AI answers?

Getting recommended in Google Gemini depends on being a recognizable entity, not just a high-ranking page. Gemini names brands it can corroborate across multiple independent sources, so consistent entity details everywhere, Organization schema linking verified profiles, and accurate third-party mentions tend to carry more weight than adding another page to the brand's own site.

How does growth-driven design work for B2B websites?

Growth-driven design treats the site as the conversion layer of a longer growth system: it launches a focused version fast, then improves the pages that carry pipeline as real buyer behavior comes in. A launchpad site of the highest-impact pages goes live in roughly 60 to 70 days instead of the four to six months a traditional B2B rebuild takes.

What is HubSpot CMS, and how does Content Hub work?

HubSpot Content Hub is HubSpot's content management system and AI-powered content marketing software for building a website and running the blogs, landing pages, and content around it in one place. It is the renamed and expanded version of what HubSpot sold as CMS Hub until an April 2024 rebrand.

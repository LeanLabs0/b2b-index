Compare questions

Where do these numbers come from

Public filings, licensed data providers, and verified company submissions, scored on five weighted factors. Each factor and its weight is defined on the methodology page, and Health is defined separately because it carries no weight in the Index Score.

Can any two companies be compared

Yes. Scores share the same 0 to 100 scale, so a cross-category comparison still reads. We recommend comparing inside one category, because that is the choice a buyer is actually making.

How often do the numbers refresh

Monthly. Every score recomputes in each snapshot, and a comparison always shows the latest one.

Top 100 questions

Is the Top 100 one overall list

No. Every ranking is scoped to a category, because a buyer choosing a marketing agency is not choosing between it and a payments platform. Scores are normalized 0 to 100 on the same five factors, so you can read a score across categories even though the ranking sits inside one.

How many companies appear in each category

Up to 100. A category with fewer than 100 indexed companies ranks all of them. Digital & AI Marketing holds 12 indexed companies today out of 50 tracked, so all 12 are ranked.

How often do these rankings update

Monthly. Every score recomputes in each snapshot.

Can a company pay to rank higher

No. The two paid programs, verification and labeled featured placement, never touch scores or rank. Featured placement is always labeled and sits outside the ranked list.

How B2Bindex Ranks Companies

Company Authority Score 25%

Scores the company across public knowledge properties such as Wikipedia, Wikidata, entities.org, and LinkedIn.

Industry Authority 20%

Benchmarks domain and product authority against other providers in the category.

Brand Reputation 20%

Aggregates verified reviews, case studies, and customer references from third-party sources.

Product Maturity (under review) 20%

Measures how developed and proven the product or service offering is. This factor's definition is under review and may change.

Funding & Operational Health 15%

Considers funding stage, runway indicators, and headcount growth as signals of stability.

The five factors combine into a composite Index Score from 0 to 100, shown to one decimal. Health is a separate operational sub-score, defined in the methodology. Reviewed by the B2Bindex editorial team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are companies selected for the index?

A company enters the index when it meets a minimum profile completeness threshold of 60%. Data comes from public filings, licensed data providers, and verified company submissions.

How often is the index updated?

Monthly. Every score recomputes in each snapshot.

How do I submit my company?

Use the Get Listed page. Basic listings are free. Submissions are reviewed by the editorial team within 5 to 7 business days.

What does a Verified Profile mean?

It means someone at the company claimed the profile and proved ownership, which lets them fill in missing data. It doesn't change the score.

How is the Index Score calculated?

Five weighted factors, shown with their weights in the module above. The composite runs 0 to 100 with one decimal.

Does a listing cost anything?

Basic listings are free. The two paid programs are profile verification and labeled featured placement.

How do I dispute or update information?

Claim your profile to correct it directly, or send the correction to the editorial team. Disputed data points are re-checked against primary sources before the next snapshot.

How do I contact the editorial team?

Through the Get Listed page, which routes both new submissions and editorial questions.

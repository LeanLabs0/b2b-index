Shortlist questions

Is the shortlist influenced by payment

No. The two paid programs, verification and labeled featured placement, never touch scores or rank, and they do not touch shortlists either. A company cannot pay to be recommended to you.

Do companies know they were shortlisted

No. Nothing is sent to a company when it appears in your shortlist, and your answers are not shared with anyone on the list. You decide who to contact.

What happens if nothing fits

You get fewer results, or none. We would rather return one honest match than pad a list to fill three slots. When that happens, widen a filter or open the full category leaderboard.
